How it works

  1. Pick a MIDI file (search "song name midi") and the part of the song you want.
  2. Choose which track is the melody, then add layers: extra bass, plus drums or harmony (max 3 instruments).
  3. Click Convert, then ▶ Minecraft preview to hear what the note blocks will sound like.
  4. Build it with Copy next: each click copies the next command and tells you the repeater delay to set.

Everything runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
