How it works

  1. Pick an MP3 and the part of the song you want.
  2. Choose the layers: the melody alone is the most recognizable. Add extra bass, plus drums or a harmony note, for a fuller sound (max 3 instruments).
  3. Click Convert, then ▶ Minecraft preview to hear what the note blocks will sound like.
  4. Build it with Copy next: each click copies the next command and tells you the repeater delay to set.

Everything runs on your PC. The song is never uploaded anywhere.
